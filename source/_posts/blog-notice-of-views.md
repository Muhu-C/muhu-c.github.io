### 一、关于域名备案与访问性能  
因本人当前年龄**未满足国内域名备案的法定**条件，本站域名 **mc118cn.cn** 暂无法在中国境内完成 ICP 备案。受此限制，本站及其附属图片存储站点均无法使用**境内 CDN 加速**服务，亦无法通过**国内注册商**进行域名续费。该限制预计于 **2028 年 8 月**解除，届时将另行通知。

### I. Regarding Domain Name Registration and Access Performance

As I have **not yet reached the statutory age** required for domestic domain name registration in China, the domain name **mc118cn.cn** cannot currently complete ICP filing within the territory of China. Due to this restriction, this website and its affiliated image hosting site are unable to use **domestic CDN acceleration** services, nor can the domain be renewed through **domestic registrars**. This restriction is expected to be lifted in **August 2028**, and a further notice will be issued at that time.

<br >

### II. Regarding Site Accessibility

Given the current network environment and the location of the hosting provider, the quality of domestic access to this site may fluctuate. According to actual speed test data, approximately **8%** of domestic hosts in China are unable to access this site. Moreover, domestic hosts in other regions of the world are able to access this site normally.

To reduce reliance on third-party image hosting services, I completed the deployment of a dedicated image storage site with the same hosting provider on the afternoon of July 30, 2026.

At present, the access availability of this image hosting site is roughly on par with that of the main site. However, it is subject to the same registration restrictions, and its access stability cannot be fully guaranteed at this time.
